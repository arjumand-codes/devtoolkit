FAQ

Text Case Converter FAQs

Common questions about uppercase, lowercase, title case, camelCase, and slug formatting.

What is a text case converter?

A text case converter changes text from one format to another, such as uppercase, lowercase, title case, camelCase, snake_case, or kebab-case.

What is title case?

Title case capitalizes the main words in a phrase or heading. It is commonly used for titles, headings, and article names.

What is camelCase used for?

camelCase is commonly used in programming for JavaScript variables, object keys, and function names.

What is slug case?

Slug case converts text into lowercase words separated by hyphens. It is useful for SEO-friendly URLs and filenames.

Is this text case converter free?

Yes, DevToolKit Text Case Converter is free and runs directly in the browser.
